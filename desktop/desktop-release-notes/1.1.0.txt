Icon titlebar (new dashboard, health, settings, downloads). Health, Settings and a persisted, live Downloads history page are overlays toggled by their icons over the focused tab, with a "No tabs open" page when no tab is open; closing a tab focuses its right (else left) neighbour. New downloads pop up a dropdown under the Downloads icon (progress ring + count badge) instead of corner cards, fading away 3 s after they finish.
